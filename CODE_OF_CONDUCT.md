# Code of conduct

Be decent. This is a small hobby project and I want it to stay a friendly place to ask questions and share ideas.

## What that looks like

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Trackmania, Python or reinforcement learning.
- Disagree about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, hate speech or sexual content.
- Don't share other people's private information.

## What happens if someone breaks it

Comments, issues and pull requests that cross the line may be edited or removed, and repeat offenders may be blocked from the repo.

## Reporting

To report someone, email joshua@laveryjonez.uk. Reports are kept private.
